FILE: neonate_body_size.csv
Description: This csv contains neonate weight, adult weight, and neonate over adult data used for the analysis. Each row represents a species, and each column represents a numerical or categorical variable.
Variables:

species
Scientific species name

neonate_body_size
Average weight of a neonate individual (grams)

body_mass_g
Average weight of an adult individual (grams)

longevity
Maximum longevity in captivity (days)

invasiveness
Placenta invasiveness type:
Epitheliochorial
Endotheliochorial
Hemochorial

interdigitation
Placenta interdigitation type:
Villous
Trabecular
Labyrinthine

neonate_over_adult
Proportion of neonate_body_size over body_mass_g.

taxonomic_order
Taxonomic order of the species
