FILE: litter_mass.csv
Description: This csv contains interbirth intervals raw data used for the analysis. Each row represents a species, and each column represents a numerical or categorical variable.

Variables:
species
Scientific species name

taxonomic_order
Taxonomic order of the species

litter_mass
Average litter mass of the species (grams)

longevity
Maximum longevity (days)

body_mass_g
Average body mass of the species (grams)

female_body_mass_g
Average female body mass of the species

invasiveness
Placenta invasiveness type:
Epitheliochorial
Endotheliochorial
Hemochorial

interdigitation
Placenta interdigitation type:
Villous
Trabecular
Labyrinthine

relative_litter_mass
Ratio of litter mass over adult body mass

relative_litter_mass_female
Ration of litter mass over female body mass

litter_size
Average litter size of the species (units)

neonate_body_size
Average neonate body size of the species (grams)
